# Open problems

The authoritative inventory of open and deliberately held work is
[`docs/OPEN_PROBLEMS.md`](docs/OPEN_PROBLEMS.md). This root document is a thin
index only; it does not duplicate, close, or relabel any inventory item.

## Honesty boundary

Open and held work remains open or held until the evidence described in the
authoritative inventory is retained. This documentation-only surface makes no
`READY` claim. A merge, passing checks, or a tip-cite records provenance only;
it does not establish readiness, benchmark quality, security, hiring outcome,
or any other result. Do not invent scores, outcomes, or closure evidence.

characters of the merge commit on `main`:
