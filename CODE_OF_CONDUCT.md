# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, useful place to discuss strategy,
software, and reproducible evidence. Participants are expected to be
respectful, assume good faith, explain disagreements clearly, and keep reports,
reviews, and other project spaces focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing
private or credential-bearing material. Do not use issues, pull requests,
logs, or benchmark artifacts to expose secrets or sensitive data.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer at
[`tnt850910@aol.com`](mailto:tnt850910@aol.com). Include the relevant link or
context and any information needed to understand what happened. Do not include
secrets in the report. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially
as practical; security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.
